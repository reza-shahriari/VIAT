## Q1. What each product/project does automatically in video and images (2025–2026 docs): discovery of entering objects, automatic track ending, low-confidence review, VLM verification

### Takeaway
No commercial or open-source *annotation tool* documents a complete unattended loop of "text prompt → discover every object that enters anywhere in the video → end tracks at exit → correct drift/ID switches → hand the human a ranked list of likely errors." Commercial platforms almost all still use **user-seeded propagation over a bounded frame range** (SAM 2/SAM 3 tracking in CVAT, Encord, Kili, Labelbox, V7, Supervisely, Label Studio). Fully automatic video tracks exist in only two forms: (a) **closed-set detector + MOT tracker** (Label Studio YOLO backend, X-AnyLabeling MOT mode, Supervisely "Apply NN to Videos"), and (b) **SAM 3's open-vocabulary detect-and-track video predictor** (native Meta repo, surfaced in X-AnyLabeling and the Ultralytics library, with V7/Encord offering class-name-prompted SAM 3 labeling). Confidence-ranked review exists mainly at the image/dataset level (FiftyOne, Label Studio, Encord, Roboflow thresholds). VLM-based *verification* is barely there: Labelbox's AI critics cover classifications only, and Encord's Task Agents are do-it-yourself.

### Cited Findings

#### CVAT (open-source MIT core; CVAT Online; CVAT Enterprise)
- **SAM 2 tracker ships in two forms:** an Enterprise-only Nuclio function, and an "AI agent" version for CVAT Online and Enterprise that runs on the user's own hardware. It tracks **polygons and masks, not rectangles**, in the AI Tools dialog. The user must draw or select the starting shape and choose a target frame; the page says nothing about auto-discovery or how a track ends on occlusion or exit. Skeletons are unsupported, and if the agent crashes its tracking state is lost — [CVAT docs: Segment Anything 2 Tracker](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/auto-annotation/segment-anything-2-tracker/)
- The AI-agent tracker is launched with `cvat-cli function run-agent` (`-p device=str:cuda` for GPU). Only one agent per function can run at a time, and tracking state lives only in agent memory. Rectangles can be tracked only through the "annotation action" path, and only with tracking functions that support rectangles — [CVAT changelog: SAM2 tracking via AI agents](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/changelog/sam2-ai-agent-tracking) (undated page)
- The Enterprise SAM 2 tracker (Enterprise Basic/Premium) works on one object per interaction. The user sets "the number of frames to track", and masks must be converted to polygons and then to Track mode before the tracker runs. The page gives no quantitative claims — [CVAT changelog: SAM 2 video tracking](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/changelog/video-annotation-sam-2)
- **Auto-annotation API (the key design fact):** detection functions return new shapes per image and take a `conf_threshold`. Tracking functions (`init_tracking_state`, `track`, optional `preprocess_image`, `TrackingFunctionSpec.supported_shape_types`) *propagate user-provided shapes* and can return `None` when they lose the target. **Tracking functions cannot discover new objects** — [CVAT SDK auto-annotation docs](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/api_sdk/sdk/auto-annotation/)
- CVAT's model kinds are detector, interactor, tracker and "reidentifier" (used during automatic annotation). The self-hosted Community edition supports only Nuclio functions. "Native" functions (detector/interactor/tracker, i.e. AI agents) are Enterprise/Online only, and third-party functions are detector-only — [CVAT docs: AI models](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/auto-annotation/ai-models/)
- Ultralytics YOLO via AI agents (classification, detection, instance segmentation, OBB, pose). The announcement does not mention tracking — [CVAT changelog: Ultralytics YOLO agentic labeling](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/changelog/ultralytics-yolo-agentic-labeling)
- **SAM 3 in CVAT is image-only so far.** Part 2 turns label names into text prompts (plus a clicked visual example) with a threshold slider. It is on paid Online plans and Enterprise; the Free tier gets demo mode only. "Part 3" is announced as "advanced video object tracking built on SAM 3's tracking capabilities" — [CVAT changelog: SAM 3 Part 2 text prompts](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/changelog/sam3-text-prompts). Search snippets date Parts 1–2 to around 26 Mar 2026 — [CVAT changelog: SAM 3 Part 1](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/changelog/sam-3-image-segmentation). I found **no Part 3 (video) release** as of 2026-09-30.
- The 2025 wrap-up lists AI Agents, Ultralytics YOLO integration, SAM 2 tracking (native for Enterprise, via agents for Online), Honeypots and Immediate Job Feedback — [CVAT Digest 2025 (23 Dec 2025)](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/changelog/digest-dec-2025)
- Video basics: keyframes with interpolation, occluded/outside handling, SAM 2, and OpenCV/TransT trackers. The guide does not say that CVAT auto-detects new objects or ends tracks by itself — [CVAT video annotation guide (24 Mar 2026)](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/blog/video-annotation-guide)
- **QA automation compares human work against ground truth, not model predictions.** It supports GT jobs, Honeypots (validation frames mixed into jobs; 5–15% suggested), precision/recall/IoU metrics and consensus — [CVAT docs: Automated QA, Review & Honeypots](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/qa-analytics/auto-qa/); [CVAT Honeypots announcement](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/changelog/annotation-qa-honeypots)

#### Label Studio (HumanSignal; Apache-2.0 Community + paid Starter Cloud/Enterprise)
- **SAM 2 video backend:** "As of 8/11/2024, SAM2 only runs on GPU servers. Currently, we only support the tracking of one object in video, although SAM2 can support multiple. Currently, we do not support video segmentation. No Docker support." Output is VideoRectangle — [label-studio-ml-backend: segment_anything_2_video README](https://github.com/HumanSignal/label-studio-ml-backend/tree/master/label_studio_ml/examples/segment_anything_2_video)
- **VideoVector tag + SAM 2** (post dated 15 Jul 2026): the user seeds an object with points or a box and SAM 2 follows it. It is available on "Starter Cloud and above" (paid) — [HumanSignal blog](https://humansignal.com/blog/video-object-segmentation-and-tracking-with-videovector-tag-for-sam-2-in-label-studio/)
- **YOLO ML backend: fully automatic tracks (closed-set).** "The first step is to detect bounding boxes, the second step is to track them (find the same boxes among frames)." It uses BoT-SORT by default or ByteTrack (`model_tracker`), with `model_conf` (default 0.25) and `model_iou`, and tracker parameters prefixed `botsort_`/`bytetrack_`. The docs warn that video tracking is computationally heavy and hits Label Studio timeouts, and recommend a CLI for long videos — [YOLO backend README](https://github.com/HumanSignal/label-studio-ml-backend/blob/master/label_studio_ml/examples/yolo/README.md); [Label Studio YOLO docs](https://labelstud.io/guide/ml_tutorials/yolo)
- **Confidence-ordered review queue (task level):** the Data Manager can sort tasks by `prediction.score` ascending, so annotators see the least-confident predictions first. Community edition does this by manual sorting; Enterprise automates the active-learning routing — [Label Studio docs: active learning](https://docs.humansignal.com/guide/active_learning)

#### X-AnyLabeling (CVHub520; GPL-3.0; desktop PyQt app, the closest analogue to VIAT)
- Models include SAM 1/2/3, Grounding DINO, YOLO-World, YOLOE, Florence-2, and VLMs (Qwen3-VL, Gemini, ChatGPT, GLM). **MOT via Bot-SORT/ByteTrack**, single-object tracking via SAM2/3-Video, interactive video segmentation, "Run all images" batch labeling, and the optional X-AnyLabeling-Server for remote inference. The README was last updated 2026-09-18 — [X-AnyLabeling README](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/blob/main/README.md)
- **MOT mode discovers objects automatically:** a detector (detection, OBB, segmentation or pose) plus TrackTrack, ByteTrack or Bot-Sort. The track ID is stored in `group_id`. The docs warn that "the first few frames may not have a `group_id` if the object confidence score is below the tracking threshold" (`track_high_thresh`). You must "Reset Tracker" between videos, and Ctrl+B batch-processes a whole video — [X-AnyLabeling MOT README](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/blob/main/examples/multiple_object_tracking/README.md)
- **SAM 3 video:** enter a text prompt such as "person", press Send, and results propagate forward. Visual prompts use positive and negative points. Output is boxes by default (`show_masks` enables masks, which is slower). It requires X-AnyLabeling-Server ≥ v0.0.4 and client ≥ v3.3.4. **"Each session supports only one category"**, and "If you cancel the propagation task midway, all processed frame results will be lost" — [X-AnyLabeling SAM3 video README](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/blob/main/examples/interactive_video_object_segmentation/sam3/README.md)
- The integrator's announcement: "if you type 'bicycle', it'll find and track every bike in the video, not just one", and the integration was optimized "for more stable memory usage and faster inference" versus the original SAM 3 — [DEV Community post](https://dev.to/jack_wang_d47b1f7f781c64f/just-added-sam3-video-object-tracking-to-x-anylabeling-33md). The feature request is dated 2025-12-28 — [issue #1258](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/issues/1258)
- No documented confidence-ranked review queue, and no documented VLM-based verification workflow (VLMs are offered as labelers and chat) — [X-AnyLabeling README](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/blob/main/README.md)

#### Roboflow (cloud; credits)
- **Auto Label** models: "GPT-6 Astra" (boxes and masks), SAM 3, Gemini (boxes), models trained in Roboflow on the same dataset, and saved Workflows. Classes carry visual text descriptions. There is a **per-class confidence threshold for SAM 3 only** ("GPT-6 Astra options and Gemini (Boxes) return every label at full confidence"). Previews are free; runs consume credits, with OpenAI tokens billed on top. Review of results is "recommended". It "works best when labeling common objects with clear visual descriptions" — [Roboflow docs: Auto Label](https://docs.roboflow.com/datasets/annotate/annotate/ai-labeling/auto-label)
- SAM 3 arrived in Auto Label in Nov 2025 — [Roboflow changelog, Nov 2025](https://docs.roboflow.com/changelog/explore-by-month/november-2025/auto-label-data-with-sam-3) — and across the Playground, inference API, edge/private-cloud deployment, Workflows and Label Assist — [Roboflow SAM 3 launch blog](https://blog.roboflow.com/sam3/)
- **No track-level video annotation:** the video workflow is to extract frames, then use image tools (Repeat Previous, Label Assist, Auto Label/Rapid). ByteTrack appears only in *inference* pipelines — [Roboflow blog: video-heavy pipelines (26 Mar 2026)](https://blog.roboflow.com/how-to-use-roboflow-for-video-heavy-pipelines/); [Roboflow blog: video annotation](https://blog.roboflow.com/video-annotation/)

#### Encord (cloud SaaS)
- "SAM 2 is used for tracking" while "SAM 3 is used for labeling objects". Per the docs, instances are **not** auto-detected across a video: the user triggers the sparkle icon or the wand with "All objects prediction". Tracking runs for **30 frames by default**, configurable. In the "Automated labeling" feature, **tracking stops if an instance is missing for more consecutive frames than the configured "tracking annotation interval"**. Supported shapes are polygon, bbox, bitmask and rotatable bbox (not keypoints or polylines) — [Encord docs: SAM segmentation & tracking](https://docs.encord.com/platform-documentation/Annotate/automated-labeling/annotate-sam)
- SAM 3 launch: a sparkles button next to each class name "uses the class name as a text prompt and labels all detected instances with one click", and the blog stresses detecting multiple objects automatically, then tracking — [Encord blog: SAM 3 in Encord](https://encord.com/blog/sam-3-in-encord/) (post text truncated on fetch; the content comes from the search snippet, and the date could not be confirmed)
- **Track-level QA metrics (rare in this market):** "Broken tracks" (frames missing inside a tracked range), "Inconsistent tracks" (high IoU between consecutive-frame objects that carry different object hashes, i.e. likely ID switches) and "Inconsistent classes", surfaced by filter/sort in Explore. The docs note that broken tracks also flag legitimate occlusions (false positives) — [Encord docs: video metrics & label validation](https://docs.encord.com/platform-documentation/Annotate/active-annotate/basics/annotate-video-metrics-label-validation)
- An "Object Annotation Quality" metric checks a label's class against its nearest neighbours in embedding space. Other metrics cover label duplicates and border proximity — [Encord blog: data/label/model quality metrics](https://encord.com/blog/data-quality-metrics/)
- **Task Agents** are workflow stages running custom code for "pre-labeling using foundation models such as GPT-4o, automated quality assurance, or any other custom action" — [Encord SDK: Task Agents](https://docs.encord.com/sdk-documentation/projects-sdk/sdk-task-agents); [encord-agents repo](https://github.com/encord-team/encord-agents)

#### V7 Darwin (cloud SaaS)
- **SAM 3 release (4 Feb 2026):** "automatic class detection based on class names": enter "car" and it will "automatically detect all instances of that class and segment them", with "enhanced auto-tracking capabilities". The entry does not say how exits are handled — [V7 changelog: SAM 3](https://www.v7darwin.com/changelog/sam-3-text-prompt-segmentation-and-auto-tracking)
- **Auto-Track** handles polygons and boxes on user-created annotations and recommends "a keyframe at the beginning and end of the label". It does **not** detect new objects. "When the object is out of frame, Auto-Track will not place a keyframe on the annotation timeline", but "The polygon or bounding box will still be present in this release" — [V7 docs: Auto-Track](https://docs.v7labs.com/docs/auto-track). Users can mark objects in or out of view manually — [V7 docs: Video labeling guide](https://docs.v7labs.com/docs/video-labeling-guide)
- SAM 2 in V7 (vendor phrasing): "Very often, you need just a single click to track objects throughout an entire video sequence" — [V7 changelog: SAM 2](https://www.v7labs.com/darwin/changelog/sam-2-now-available-in-v7)

#### Supervisely (platform + app ecosystem)
- Video tracking families: SOT (MixFormer, TransT; class-agnostic from a first-frame template), MOT (YOLOv5 + DeepSort, tracking-by-detection) and VOS (SAM 2, XMem, mask-based), plus CoTracker and PIPs — [Supervisely docs: video tracking](https://docs.supervisely.com/labeling/labeling-toolbox/video-tracking)
- **"Apply NN to Videos Project" app:** "Predictions on every frame are combined with BoT-SORT/DeepSort into tracks automatically". The app is AGPL-3.0 and prefers a GPU — [Supervisely ecosystem app](https://ecosystem.supervisely.com/apps/apply-nn-to-videos-project)
- **Auto Track app:** objects are tracked after the user creates or edits them. For exits: "If an object has left the scene, you can delete it permanently" via "Delete all from current". No new-object discovery — [auto-track README](https://github.com/supervisely-ecosystem/auto-track). *Conflict:* a Supervisely page surfaced in search claims Auto Tracking "can also stop tracking when an object leaves the scene" (I could not verify which page: [Videos 3.0 docs](https://docs.supervisely.com/labeling/labeling-toolbox/videos-3.0) / [Video Annotation 3.0 blog](https://supervisely.com/blog/video-annotation-update-2024/)).
- SAM 2 powers the "NN Image Labeling" automatic masks and a "Batched Smart Tool" (box → mask in batch). The free Community version includes a running SAM 2 Smart Tool — [Supervisely SAM 2 blog](https://supervisely.com/blog/segment-anything-2-for-automatically-segment-and-track-objects/)
- A "Serve Segment Anything 3" app (released 5 Dec 2025, updated 18 Sep 2026; GPU required; tagged images and videos) exists, but the page does not document text-prompted video tracking — [Supervisely SAM 3 app](https://ecosystem.supervisely.com/apps/sam3/serve)

#### SuperAnnotate (cloud SaaS)
- Autotrack (docs) runs in the Image Editor on **Point-tool points** across 1–20 items (default 10), triggered with `T`. Revert is only available immediately afterwards — [SuperAnnotate docs: Autotrack](https://doc.superannotate.com/docs/image-autotrack). The video docs cover manual bbox/polygon/polyline/point/event instances with keyframes and a timeline; I found no AI tracking documented there — [SuperAnnotate docs: video annotation](https://doc.superannotate.com/docs/video-annotation). The marketing page says Autotrack predicts "the movement of one or multiple moving objects" — [SuperAnnotate video page](https://www.superannotate.com/video-annotation)

#### Labelbox (cloud SaaS)
- 2 Sep 2025: SAM2 in the video editor — click points on one frame, pick a frame range, and masks are added over that range. 4 Mar 2026: the video editor moved to SAM3. 7 May 2024: Foundry video predictions via GroundingDINO (boxes) and GroundingDINO+SAM (masks). 4 May 2026: "AI critics" in the audio/video editors for classification types. 5 Jun 2024: Benchmarks and Consensus can be used together — [Labelbox release notes](https://docs.labelbox.com/changelog)

#### Kili Technology (cloud SaaS)
- **SAM 2 Smart Tracking (beta, 6 Nov 2024):** the user makes a mask, and the target is then segmented "automatically over the next 50 frames". "If SAM2 loses track of the target, it will stop tracking, and the object will no longer appear in subsequent frames." A single target per session is recommended — [Kili changelog 6 Nov 2024](https://docs.kili-technology.com/changelog/november-6th-2024)
- Kili documents a "Group" feature for re-appearing objects that were labeled as different items (e.g. after ML pre-annotation), to merge them on the timeline — [Kili docs: labeling video assets](https://docs.kili-technology.com/docs/labeling-video-assets) (fetched via search snippet; the page timed out)

#### Segments.ai, Dataloop, T-Rex Label
- Segments.ai focuses on multi-sensor (lidar/camera) data: label a cuboid once and it propagates across frames, with pause-and-correct on deviations and consistent IDs across sensors — [Segments.ai home](https://segments.ai/); [Segments.ai multi-sensor blog](https://segments.ai/blog/multi-sensor-data-labeling-for-robotics-and-av/). I found no documented 2D text-prompt video discovery.
- Dataloop: "Select the object you wish to track, provide it with an annotation label, turn on the video tracker" to predict future frames. Nothing else is documented on that page — [Dataloop automatic video tracking](https://dataloop.ai/video/automatic-video-tracking/)
- T-Rex Label is browser-based with no install. Its visual prompt ("draw a bounding box around an object and it will detect the rest of the similar objects"), batch propagation across images and AI Mask are documented; video tracking is not — [T-Rex Label](https://trexlabel.com/en/). Pre-annotation reportedly uses DINO-X / Grounding DINO 1.6 text prompts — [T-Rex Label glossary: DINO-X](https://trexlabel.com/en/glossary/396/). Those models need a cloud API token — [Grounded-SAM-2 README](https://github.com/IDEA-Research/Grounded-SAM-2/blob/main/README.md)

#### Voxel51 FiftyOne (Apache-2.0 open source + Enterprise)
- **Verified Auto Labeling (VAL)** is an Enterprise feature, in beta for existing Enterprise customers per the docs snippet. It advertises "one-click QA" (accept or reject auto-labels with confidence scoring), "intelligent ranking for human review" and "difficulty scoring" — [FiftyOne docs: Auto-Labeling (Enterprise)](https://docs.voxel51.com/enterprise/verified_auto_labeling.html) (HTTP 403 to fetcher; content from search snippet); [Voxel51 VAL event (24 Jun 2025)](https://voxel51.com/events/verified-auto-labeling-smarter-annotation-at-scale-june-24-2025)
- The annotation product page describes "Intelligent Review", which "uses embeddings, model predictions, and auto-label comparison to surface annotation errors at scale", with highest-risk labels first. It also lists video annotation with interpolation and SAM 2 tracking — [voxel51.com/annotation](https://voxel51.com/annotation)
- **Open-source mistakenness:** `compute_mistakenness()` scores label errors against predictions. It marks `missing` (confident predictions with no matching ground truth) and `spurious` (ground-truth objects likely wrong), and computes `mistakenness_loc` for box-localization errors — [fiftyone.brain API](https://docs.voxel51.com/api/fiftyone.brain.html); [FiftyOne tutorial: finding detection mistakes](https://docs.voxel51.com/tutorials/detection_mistakes.html)

#### Ultralytics (AGPL-3.0 library; Ultralytics Platform cloud)
- **Platform annotation editor:** SAM smart annotation from "SAM 2.1 Tiny" (75 MB) to "SAM 3.1" (3.50 GB), with auto-apply. YOLO "Predict" uses confidence 0.25 and IoU 0.7 by default and **skips predictions overlapping existing annotations at IoU ≥ 0.7**. Class-prompted models (1–200 classes) include Moondream 3.1 (default), hosted "SAM 3 Text"/"SAM 3.1 Text", and nine paid vision-provider APIs. Batch Annotation costs **$1.00 per 1,000 images**. Video is not addressed — [Ultralytics docs: Platform annotation](https://docs.ultralytics.com/platform/data/annotation)
- `auto_annotate(data, det_model, sam_model, device, output_dir)` runs detector boxes through SAM and writes YOLO segmentation labels. It works on image folders only and returns nothing — [Ultralytics docs: simple utilities](https://docs.ultralytics.com/usage/simple-utilities)
- The library offers `SAM3SemanticPredictor` for images and `SAM3VideoPredictor`/**`SAM3VideoSemanticPredictor`**, which can "track all instances of concepts specified by text across video frames". Weights are gated on Hugging Face. At fetch time the docs said SAM 3.1 Object Multiplex was not supported in Ultralytics — [Ultralytics docs: SAM 3](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/sam-3/). A user request "Integrated sam3.1" was opened 30 Mar 2026 and closed 11 Sep 2026; the closure reason is not visible in the issue body — [ultralytics#24059](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/issues/24059)

#### Open research pipelines that auto-discover objects in video (local, code available)
- **SAM 3 (Meta), the most important building block.** "On each frame, the detector finds new objects … and the tracker propagates masklets"; matching is "a simple IoU based matching function"; "We further spawn new masklets for all newly detected objects that are not matched". "If a masklet's detection score falls below a threshold, we suppress it" (the score measures how consistently it was matched to detections over a temporal window). "We periodically re-prompt the tracker with high-confidence detection masks … replacing the tracker's own predictions". Stated limits: "inference latency scales with the number of objects, sustaining near real-time performance for ∼5 concurrent objects"; "The merging might suffer from ambiguities, especially in crowded scenes"; reaches "over 80% of human pHOTA" — [SAM 3 paper (arXiv 2511.16719, 20 Nov 2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16719)
- The SAM 3 repo takes JPEG folders or MP4 and needs CUDA ≥ 12.6 and PyTorch ≥ 2.7. **SAM 3.1 Object Multiplex (27 Mar 2026)** is "a shared-memory approach for joint multi-object tracking that is significantly faster without sacrificing accuracy" — [facebookresearch/sam3 README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3)
- **Grounded-SAM-2 "continuous ID" demos** "try to **find new objects** and assign them with new ID across the whole video, this function is **still under develop**. it's not that stable now". Detection runs every `step` frames. The "plus" version adds reverse tracking so it "could simply cover the whole lifetime of the object". DINO-X and Grounding DINO 1.5/1.6 need API tokens; original Grounding DINO and Florence-2 run locally — [Grounded-SAM-2 README](https://github.com/IDEA-Research/Grounded-SAM-2/blob/main/README.md)
- **DEVA:** image-level detection every N frames (`detection_every`) with "in-clip consensus" to suppress false positives, plus bi-directional propagation. Objects missed for more than `max_missed_detection_count` are deleted. Caveats: "Positive detections are amplified temporally due to propagation", and high object turnover (e.g. driving scenes) causes memory issues — [DEVA README](https://github.com/hkchengrex/Tracking-Anything-with-DEVA)
- **SAM-Track** tracks and segments objects "either automatically or interactively" (SAM on keyframes plus AOT propagation) — [Segment-and-Track-Anything repo](https://github.com/z-x-yang/Segment-and-Track-Anything)
- **SAM2Auto** (arXiv, 9 Jun 2025) claims to be the "first fully automated annotation pipeline for video datasets", with "no human intervention or dataset-specific training" (SMART-OD detection plus FLASH tracking with statistical false-positive handling). I found no code link — [arXiv 2506.07850](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07850)
- **SAMannot** (arXiv, 16 Jan 2026) is a local, open-source SAM2 video instance-segmentation tool with a modified SAM2 for low memory, "persistent instance identity management", a "lock-and-refine workflow with barrier frames" and skeleton-based auto-prompting — [arXiv 2601.11301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11301)

### Inferences
- **The 2025–26 commercial state of the art is still assistive, not unattended.** Every platform's documented SAM-based video tracking needs a human seed and a frame range or target frame (Encord 30 frames by default, Kili 50, Labelbox a frame range, CVAT a target frame, V7 keyframes). Several limit you to one object or category per session (Label Studio SAM 2 backend, Kili, CVAT Enterprise SAM 2 per interaction, X-AnyLabeling SAM 3 per category).
- **"Auto-end at exit" is handled explicitly by only a few:** Kili (stop and remove on lost target), Encord's automated labeling (missing-frames interval), SAM 3 (masklet suppression) and DEVA (`max_missed_detection_count`). V7 leaves shapes present when out of frame, and Supervisely's Auto Track tells users to delete manually.
- **Only Encord documents track-level error signals** (broken tracks, inconsistent tracks meaning likely ID switches, inconsistent classes). FiftyOne and cleanlab-style scoring work per object or image. For VIAT's "small ranked set of likely errors" goal, a track-level scorer would have to be built, modelled on Encord's metrics plus FiftyOne-style mistakenness.
- **VLMs are used almost only as labelers** (Roboflow GPT-6 Astra/Gemini, Ultralytics Moondream/paid APIs, X-AnyLabeling Qwen3-VL/Gemini). The documented VLM "verification" I found is Labelbox AI critics (classifications only) and custom Encord Task Agents. I found no documented VLM verification of boxes or tracks in video.
- For VIAT (local, desktop, drone footage), the realistic "already solved" pieces are SAM 3's detect-and-track loop (discovery, ending, drift re-prompting) and detector+MOT trackers. Neither comes with a triage layer, and both have scaling issues on dense small-object scenes (see Q3).

### Gaps
- Whether Encord's and V7's SAM 3 features run *whole-video* discovery (detect on every frame, spawn new tracks) or only single-frame detection followed by tracking is not documented. The Encord SAM 3 blog fetch was truncated.
- The FiftyOne VAL docs returned HTTP 403, and the Enterprise docs source is not in the public repo, so details (video support, the exact review UI) come from search snippets and marketing pages.
- Segments.ai, Dataloop and SuperAnnotate have little public documentation on AI video tracking; their QA/review automation was not researched in depth.
- CVAT's "reidentifier" model kind is documented, but whether it merges per-frame detections into tracks in current releases is not.

## Q2. Reported time savings / accuracy numbers (vendor claims vs independent evidence)

### Takeaway
Vendor numbers are mostly unexplained multipliers (Encord "6x", Kili "10x", FiftyOne "80% speed-up", Voxel51 "100,000x cheaper"). The only detailed, reproducible study found (Voxel51's, image-only) shows auto-labels reach about 93–95% of human-label downstream mAP on VOC/COCO but only about 62–68% on the domain-shifted BDD and long-tail LVIS. Low confidence thresholds (0.2) worked best. Independent evidence on hybrid video workflows is modest (about 35% time saved for most users in one small study). I found no independent head-to-head benchmark of commercial auto-tracking on drone footage.

### Cited Findings
- **Vendor claim (Voxel51 blog, Jun 2025):** VAL "can cut costs by 100,000x and reach 95% of human performance"; models trained only on auto-labels reached "90–95%" of human-label accuracy "in many cases" — [Voxel51 blog](https://voxel51.com/blog/zero-shot-auto-labeling-rivals-human-performance)
- **Vendor-authored research (arXiv 2506.02359, 3 Jun 2025; Griffin, Gangwar, Sela, Corso; published via Voxel51's blog):** YOLO11n was trained on YOLO-World auto-labels at confidence α = 0.2. mAP50 as a share of the human-label result: VOC 0.715 vs 0.756 (94.6%), COCO 0.460 vs 0.496 (92.7%), LVIS 0.059 vs 0.087 (67.8%), BDD 0.271 vs 0.434 (62.4%). "α=0.2 results in the best AL recall for all rows, best mAP50 for 9 rows"; "α=0.8 has highest precision but worst model performance in every row". Cost and time: 6,703 human hours vs 1.27 h of auto-labeling; $1.18 vs $124,092.54 for an annotation service — "approximately 15K times faster than human labeling at 1/100K the cost". Limits: BDD viewpoint shift gave the largest gap; ambiguous classes (e.g. "rider") were "left entirely unlabeled by some AL models regardless of α"; Grounding DINO was dropped for LVIS — [arXiv 2506.02359 (HTML)](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.02359); [abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02359)
- **Vendor claim (Voxel51 annotation page):** "80% speed up" with agentic labeling, "near-human accuracy" for foundation-model labels — [voxel51.com/annotation](https://voxel51.com/annotation)
- **Vendor claim (Encord):** video page titled "6x Faster Video Labeling" — [encord.com/video](https://encord.com/video/)
- **Vendor claim (Kili):** "annotate videos 10x faster with SAM 2 segmentation and smart object tracking", "100K+ frames, 4K video", "automated quality controls" — [Kili video annotation tool page](https://kili-technology.com/product/video-annotation-tool)
- **Vendor claim (V7):** "Very often, you need just a single click to track objects throughout an entire video sequence" — [V7 changelog: SAM 2](https://www.v7labs.com/darwin/changelog/sam-2-now-available-in-v7)
- CVAT's SAM 2 announcement gives no quantitative claims — [CVAT changelog: SAM 2](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/changelog/video-annotation-sam-2)
- **Model-level (Meta, self-reported):** SAM 3 reaches "over 80% of human pHOTA" on its video benchmarks, and is near real-time only up to about 5 concurrent objects — [SAM 3 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16719). SAM 3.1 Multiplex is described as "significantly faster without sacrificing accuracy" — [sam3 README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3); Ultralytics' docs cite a "7× speedup at scale" for SAM 3.1 — [Ultralytics SAM 3 docs](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/sam-3/)
- **Independent (academic user study):** a Human-in-the-Loop workflow with automatic pre-annotations on "high-ambiguity spatiotemporal video footage" "reduced annotation time by 35% for the majority (72%) of the participants" (18 volunteers). The authors note manual annotation remains "the gold standard" for dense temporal video datasets — [arXiv 2510.21798 (Oct 2025, rev. Feb 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21798)
- **Independent (remote sensing, 10 Jul 2026):** a zero/one-shot evaluation of SAM 3 found "sub-pixel resolution limits", "overhead semantic blind spots", and text prompts that introduce "misaligned, ground-level semantic bias, actively degrading coordinate regression". The authors recommend geospatial fine-tuning — [arXiv 2607.09583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09583)
- **Vendor blog (annotation vendor Labellerr):** "SAM 3 consistently outperforms SAM across every task" on aerial datasets, zero-shot — [Labellerr aerial benchmark](https://www.labellerr.com/blog/sam-vs-sam-3-aerial-segmentation-benchmark/)
- **Author claims (unreviewed):** SAM2Auto "achieves comparable accuracy to manual annotation while dramatically reducing annotation time and eliminating labor costs" — [arXiv 2506.07850](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07850)

### Inferences
- Vendor speed-ups cannot be compared: none disclose baseline, dataset, object density or review effort. Treat them as marketing.
- The Voxel51 data suggests the auto-label "sweet spot" for training is **high recall at a low threshold**. A no-human pipeline therefore gives up precision, so VIAT's review queue should target false positives admitted by low thresholds as well as misses. The large drops on domain-shifted data (BDD) point to a similar gap for aerial/VisDrone data, which the remote-sensing evaluation of SAM 3 supports.
- SAM 3's near-real-time limit of about 5 objects matters directly for VisDrone-style scenes with dozens to hundreds of small objects: throughput will fall well below real time unless SAM 3.1 multiplexing or tiling or chunking is used.

### Gaps
- No independent, published benchmark compares CVAT/Encord/V7/Kili/Labelbox auto-tracking (accuracy or human minutes per video) on the same footage, and none targets drone/small-object video.
- There are no public numbers on the precision/recall of confidence-ranked review queues (how many errors a top-k review catches) from any vendor.

## Q3. User complaints and gaps: where auto-tracking breaks in practice and what users still do by hand

### Takeaway
The recurring failure points are: (1) **GPU memory growth on long videos** in SAM 2/SAM 3 video predictors, with chunking breaking ID continuity; (2) **dense or crowded scenes**, where adding one new object forces resets, SAM 3 merging becomes ambiguous and there is a 200-query cap per image; (3) **single-object or single-category sessions** that force many passes; (4) **ID bookkeeping** (merging re-appearing objects, tracks split across jobs, assigning distinct IDs); and (5) **fragile ML-backend integrations** that silently do nothing or crash when no detections appear. By hand, users still seed objects, choose frame ranges, re-prompt after drift or occlusion, delete tracks at exits and merge or relabel IDs.

### Cited Findings
**Long videos / GPU memory**
- SAM 3 video OOM on videos longer than the demo: `predictor._ALL_INFERENCE_STATES` grows each loop, and `offload_video_to_cpu=True` plus async frame loading "reduced memory usage, but not enough for longer videos" (20 Nov 2025) — [sam3#169](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/169)
- "When tracking long videos, a large amount of data is cached, which can lead to very high video memory usage over time" (18 Mar 2026) — [sam3#495](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/495); related: memory not fully released — [sam3#305](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/305); reducing memory in streaming inference — [sam3#413](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/413); request for a streaming SAM 3 (19 comments) — [sam3#408](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/408)
- **Chunking breaks continuity:** a user tracking humans in long videos on consumer GPUs had to split them into batches, and "some objects that have been detected in batch 1, are lost at the transition with batch 2. the tracking context is lost" (16 Dec 2025) — [sam3#354](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/354)
- SAM 2 (Ultralytics) on videos longer than 20 minutes: "The model only works for the first 6 or 7 seconds of the video, then fails" with OOM (Dec 2024) — [ultralytics#18211](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/issues/18211). SAM 2 upstream: a 2-hour video question — [sam2#264](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2/issues/264); a report of about 60 GB of GPU memory for a 2:30 clip at 24 fps, about 21 GB even with CPU offload (per search snippet) — [sam2#196](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2/issues/196); GPU memory not released between videos — [sam2#258](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2/issues/258)

**Dense / crowded / small-object scenes**
- X-AnyLabeling (translated from Chinese): in dense scenes with SAM2, when a new target appears you must clear all targets on the current frame and reset the tracker. With "dozens or hundreds" of targets, each must be re-annotated and its ID re-aligned with previous frames. Copying the previous frame's labels does not make SAM2 track them (16 Jan 2025) — [X-AnyLabeling#801](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/issues/801)
- SAM 3 has `num_queries = 200` in the decoder, and a user asks whether that caps objects per image at 200 (raising it breaks weight loading) (17 Feb 2026) — [sam3#464](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/464). The SAM 3 paper itself notes merging ambiguities "especially in crowded scenes" — [SAM 3 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16719)
- Aerial/remote-sensing weaknesses of SAM 3 (sub-pixel limits, overhead blind spots, text-prompt bias) — [arXiv 2607.09583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09583)
- DEVA's authors: positive detections are "amplified temporally", and high object turnover causes memory issues — [DEVA README](https://github.com/hkchengrex/Tracking-Anything-with-DEVA)

**Quality / reproducibility gaps**
- "Segmentation quality in web playground is much better than local sam3.pt with same video & text prompt" (2 Dec 2025) — [sam3#275](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/275)
- `SAM3VideoSemanticPredictor` "silently loses all tracking with an incompatible SAM3 checkpoint" (13 Jul 2026) — [ultralytics#25152](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/issues/25152); video vs image predictor results differ (18 comments, Mar 2026) — [ultralytics#23941](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/issues/23941); a request to accept sequential NumPy frames without a video file (Aug 2026) — [ultralytics#25707](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/issues/25707)
- SAM 3.1 multiplex bug: "removing a shared-conditioning-frame 'owner' wipes other objects' memory" (3 Jun 2026) — [sam3#572](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/572)
- Hugging Face gating: a user asks for a rejected SAM 3 access request to be reset (20 Sep 2026) — [sam3#624](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/issues/624)

**Seeding / single-object limits / fragile integrations**
- Label Studio: "SAM 2 video backend does not actually do any work" — the spinner shows but nothing happens (Feb 2025) — [label-studio-ml-backend#701](https://github.com/HumanSignal/label-studio-ml-backend/issues/701); "Issue running SAM 2 Video tracking" — [#645](https://github.com/HumanSignal/label-studio-ml-backend/issues/645); "Can't run Video Object Tracking SAM-2 pre-annotation" — [label-studio#6827](https://github.com/HumanSignal/label-studio/issues/6827). The backend itself supports only one object — [README](https://github.com/HumanSignal/label-studio-ml-backend/tree/master/label_studio_ml/examples/segment_anything_2_video)
- X-AnyLabeling SAM2 video: objects annotated on the first frame were not propagated to the rest — [#690](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/issues/690); "only the frame marked by itself has segmentation" (Jul 2025) — [#1054](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/issues/1054); a performance bottleneck on large datasets — [#1062](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/issues/1062)
- Grounded-SAM-2 continuous-ID crashes when a frame has zero detections for the prompt — [Grounded-SAM-2#43](https://github.com/IDEA-Research/Grounded-SAM-2/issues/43); "No points are provided" during reverse tracking — [#53](https://github.com/IDEA-Research/Grounded-SAM-2/issues/53)
- CVAT: "TransT tracking not working" (Jul 2025) — [cvat#9602](https://github.com/cvat-ai/cvat/issues/9602); a SAM first click on GPU takes 6–10 s (per search snippet) — [cvat#7793](https://github.com/cvat-ai/cvat/issues/7793). The CVAT SAM 2 agent loses tracking state on crash, and one agent runs per function — [CVAT SAM2 agent changelog](https://www.cvat.ai/resources/changelog/sam2-ai-agent-tracking)

**ID management**
- CVAT: a car tracked in job 1 (frames 0–40) and job 2 (40–90) is not merged into one track in MOT export (Nov 2024) — [cvat#8629](https://github.com/cvat-ai/cvat/issues/8629)
- X-AnyLabeling: how to give two drones, both labeled "airplane", distinct tracking IDs (Aug 2025) — [#1074](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/issues/1074); MOT `group_id` missing on early frames below `track_high_thresh` — [MOT README](https://github.com/CVHub520/X-AnyLabeling/blob/main/examples/multiple_object_tracking/README.md)
- Kili's "Group" feature exists specifically because re-appearing objects end up as separate objects after pre-annotation — [Kili docs](https://docs.kili-technology.com/docs/labeling-video-assets)
- Encord's broken-track metric also flags genuine occlusions (false alarms) — [Encord docs](https://docs.encord.com/platform-documentation/Annotate/active-annotate/basics/annotate-video-metrics-label-validation)
- Practitioner guidance from annotation-vendor blogs (text from a search snippet; which of the two pages it came from is not confirmed): occlusion is "the most common source of propagation failure" in SAM 2-assisted video annotation. After a long occlusion SAM 2 resumes from the last memory state and may drift, so teams "build an explicit re-prompt protocol" — [Labellerr blog: SAM 2 video annotation](https://www.labellerr.com/blog/sam-2-video-annotation/); [Scematics blog: video annotation at scale](https://scematics.io/resource/blogs/video-annotation-at-scale-frame-by-frame-to-sam-2)

### Inferences
- A VIAT unattended pipeline must plan for **memory-bounded chunking with explicit cross-chunk ID stitching** (sam3#354 shows naive chunking loses identities). It needs **per-session object caps or tiling** for dense aerial scenes (X-AnyLabeling#801, sam3#464, SAM 3's ~5-object real-time limit), and **persistent on-disk tracking state** (CVAT's agent-memory design loses state on crash).
- The manual steps users still perform (seeding, re-prompting after occlusion, deleting at exit, merging IDs) are exactly the ones a detect-every-frame + track + track-level-QA design removes. The complaints show existing tools have not closed these steps for dense or long footage.
- Silent failures (ML backends doing nothing, checkpoint mismatches that drop all tracks) argue for health checks in any automated run, e.g. alerting when tracks drop to zero or detections vanish.

### Gaps
- Reddit (r/computervision, r/MachineLearning) could not be fetched, so first-hand forum complaints about commercial tools (pricing, lock-in, video UX) are not captured. Review sites (G2/Capterra) were not examined.
- I found no issue-tracker evidence for commercial SaaS tools (their bug trackers are private), so their practical failure modes rely on documentation limits only.

### Inferences
*(General license reading, not legal advice.)*
- **Safe to embed or vendor in a distributed desktop app (keep notices; Apache adds NOTICE and patent terms):** SAM 2, Grounded-SAM-2, GroundingDINO, MASA, roboflow/trackers, the original ByteTrack/BoT-SORT/OC-SORT, supervision, SAHI, ensemble-boxes, cleanlab (≥ the Dec 2025 relicense; older AGPL releases stay AGPL), cleanvision, FiftyOne + brain, autodistill core, rf-detr, Cutie, SAMURAI, CVAT/Label Studio code, Datumaro, DIVE.
- **SAM 3 is usable but not "open source" in the OSI sense.** Shipping its code or weights inside a VIAT bundle means passing the SAM License on to users. The military/ITAR/espionage end-use ban is directly relevant if VIAT's drone/aerial annotation serves defense customers. The gated Hugging Face weights also suggest having users download weights themselves rather than bundling them.
- **Copyleft (GPL-3.0/AGPL-3.0):** VIAT's current pattern of optional, user-installed `ultralytics` keeps VIAT's own code separate. But a PyInstaller bundle that *includes* Ultralytics, BoxMOT, YOLOE, SAM-Track, YOLO-World or X-AnyLabeling code would make the distributed bundle subject to GPL/AGPL terms. Copying X-AnyLabeling source (GPL-3.0) into VIAT would do the same. Internal, undistributed use creates no copyleft obligation (AGPL's network clause also matters only if the tool is offered as a network service). Because VIAT currently declares no license, the project owner should pick one before borrowing copyleft code.
- **Non-commercial (DEVA, CoTracker, T-Rex2):** reimplement the published ideas instead (e.g. DEVA's detect-every-N + in-clip consensus + `max_missed_detection_count`) rather than importing the code, if VIAT may be used commercially.
- **Suggested permissive shortlist mapped to VIAT pain points:**
  - *Discovery:* SAM 3 video predictor, and/or a detector (RF-DETR, Grounding DINO, or VIAT's existing optional YOLO) + SAHI/`InferenceSlicer` tiling for small objects + a Grounded-SAM-2-style "detect every N frames, spawn unmatched" loop.
  - *Track end, drift and ID switches:* SAM 3 masklet suppression and re-prompting; `trackers` (BoT-SORT/McByte camera-motion compensation for drones, OC-SORT lost-track recovery); MASA for appearance association.
  - *Confidence and verification signals:* WBF agreement across models/TTA; cleanlab `object_detection` and FiftyOne `compute_mistakenness` for ranking likely errors.
  - *Track-level QA:* reimplement Encord-style broken-track / inconsistent-track / inconsistent-class checks (the concept, not code).

### Gaps
- The licenses of individual autodistill base-model plugins and of Encord's `encord-agents` library were not checked.
- SAMannot's and SAM2Auto's code licenses and availability are unknown (the arXiv pages show only CC-BY-4.0 for the papers).
- Whether Ultralytics offers a commercial (non-AGPL) license was not verified in this session.
- Track-Anything is MIT but builds on XMem and E2FGVI, whose licenses were not checked.

## Q5. Comparison matrix (tool × capability)

### Takeaway
No row combines automatic discovery in video, automatic track ending, drift handling, a confidence-ranked review queue, AI verification, local/offline operation and a permissive license. The closest local combinations are: **X-AnyLabeling** (GPL-3.0: SAM 3 video plus detector MOT, but no review ranking); **Label Studio + YOLO backend** (Apache UI but AGPL Ultralytics; closed-set; prediction-score sorting); **FiftyOne** (Apache: review and mistakenness, but no video auto-tracking of its own); and **raw building blocks** (SAM 3 native, or detector + trackers), which cover discovery, ending and drift but have no triage layer. VIAT would be combining pieces nobody ships together.

### Inferences
- **Auto-discovery in video** is shipped in end-user tools only as closed-set detector + tracker (Label Studio, Supervisely, X-AnyLabeling, the Ultralytics library) or as SAM 3 text-prompt tracking (X-AnyLabeling, the Ultralytics library, and V7/Encord in a user-triggered form).
- **Automatic track end** is explicit only in Kili, Encord (interval rule), SAM 3 and DEVA. In detector+tracker setups it is implicit (the tracker's lost-track buffer), and to my knowledge no tool surfaces it as a reviewable event.
- **Confidence-ranked review** exists (Label Studio, FiftyOne, Encord), but at task, image or object level. Encord's track metrics are the only documented track-level ranking.
- **Local + permissive + automatic video** is available only as building blocks. This supports the conclusion that VIAT would be combining existing pieces (SAM 3 or detector + trackers + a triage layer) rather than reimplementing a solved product.

### Gaps
- Pricing tiers for Encord, V7, SuperAnnotate, Labelbox, Kili, Segments.ai and Dataloop, and on-prem/air-gapped availability for most commercial platforms, were not verified in this session. Those cells are marked "?" or "Commercial".
- The matrix reflects *documented* behavior. Undocumented UI behavior (e.g. whether V7's or Encord's SAM 3 spawns new tracks mid-video) could differ in practice.
